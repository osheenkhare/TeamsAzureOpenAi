## Current Requirement for Drafting Contract Responses

Look into the last message in the conversation thread and generate a draft response that addresses the discussion while adhering to contract management guidelines.

You can use the available MCP tools to gather necessary information for drafting the response.


## Hard rules
In the response do not mention `Draft` or indicate that it is a draft.
Do not include any disclaimers or notes about the response being a draft.
Do not ask for any approval.


## Output
